The Philosophy

SAS vs BAS

Straight Arm Strength (SAS): The defining characteristic of mastery. Elbows locked. Load transfers through connective tissue.
Examples: Planche, Front Lever, Iron Cross.

Bent Arm Strength (BAS): Traditional muscle hypertrophy. Muscle belly lengthens/shortens.
Examples: Pushups, Rows, Curls.

Key Concepts

  • Compression: Active folding of the body (L-Sit). Requires hip flexor strength at short range.
  • Hollow Body: Anti-extension. Ribs down, pelvis tucked. The "Master Shape."
  • Scapular Rhythm:
    - Handstand: Elevation
    - Planche: Protraction + Depression
    - Front Lever: Retraction + Depression

Periodization

  • Wk 1-4 (Accumulation): Master form. Keep 2 reps in reserve.
  • Wk 5-8 (Intensification): Harder leverage (Tuck -> Adv Tuck). Add weight.
  • Wk 9-11 (Peak): Test limits. Full Pistols. Muscle-up transitions.
  • Wk 12 (Deload): 50% volume. Crucial for tendon health.
