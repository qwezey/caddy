Your 5-Day At-Home Workout Program

A balanced, full-body plan for strength, cardio, and flexibility.

Program Principles

📊 Weekly Structure Overview

This program uses a "Push, Pull, Legs & Conditioning" split to ensure you train your entire body effectively throughout the week while allowing for adequate recovery. This method is superior to training one muscle group per day, as it increases training frequency and accelerates learning for beginners.

dumbbell Maximizing 1 lb Dumbbells

The light dumbbells are not for heavy lifting, but for precision. They help build a strong mind-muscle connection, provide feedback to improve your form (proprioception), and add a slight metabolic challenge to cardio movements without compromising technique.

📈 Progressive Overload

To keep making progress without heavier weights, you can:

  • Increase Time Under Tension: Slow down the lowering phase of each rep.
  • Increase Density: Do more work in less time by reducing rest periods.
  • Increase Volume: Add more reps or an extra set.
  • Increase Complexity: Move to a more challenging version of an exercise.

Dynamic Warm-Up (5-7 Mins)

Perform this routine before every workout to increase blood flow and prepare your muscles and nervous system for the session.

Jumping Jacks: 30 seconds. Jump feet out while raising arms overhead.
Arm Circles: 20 seconds forward, 20 seconds backward.
Torso Twists: 30 seconds. Twist your upper body side to side.
High Knees: 30 seconds. Run in place, driving knees toward your chest.
Butt Kicks: 30 seconds. Run in place, bringing heels toward your glutes.
Bodyweight Squats: 10 repetitions.
Inchworms: 5 repetitions. Walk hands out to a plank, then feet in.

Exercise Instructions

Push-Up

Start in a high plank, hands wider than shoulders. Lower your chest to the floor, then push up. Keep body in a straight line. Modify by placing knees on the floor.

Dumbbell Shoulder Press

Sit or stand holding dumbbells at shoulder height, palms forward. Press weights overhead until arms are extended. Lower with control.

Triceps Dip

Sit on a chair's edge, hands gripping beside hips. Slide hips forward. Lower your body by bending elbows to 90 degrees, then press up.

Dumbbell Lateral Raise

Stand holding dumbbells at sides. With a slight elbow bend, raise arms out to the sides until parallel with the floor. Lower with control.

Plank

Hold a position on forearms and toes, body forming a straight line. Engage core and glutes; don't let hips sag.

Bicycle Crunch

Lie on back, hands behind head. Twist to bring opposite elbow to opposite knee, alternating sides in a pedaling motion.

Jump Rope: Regular Bounce

Jump with both feet, using wrists to turn the rope. Land softly. Try 30s on, 30s off for 5 mins.

Exercise Instructions

Dumbbell Squat

Stand holding dumbbells at sides. Lower hips back and down as if sitting, keeping chest up. Push through heels to return to start.

Reverse Dumbbell Lunge

Step one foot far back, lowering hips until both knees are at 90-degree angles. Push off back foot to return to start. Alternate legs.

Glute Bridge

Lie on back, knees bent, feet flat. Squeeze glutes to lift hips until your body is in a straight line from shoulders to knees. Lower with control.

Dumbbell Calf Raise

Stand holding dumbbells. Slowly rise onto your tiptoes, squeezing calves at the top. Lower with control.

Jump Squat

From the bottom of a squat, explode upward into a jump. Land softly with bent knees, immediately going into the next squat.

Exercise Instructions

Dumbbell Renegade Row

In a plank with hands on dumbbells, pull one dumbbell to your chest, keeping hips square. Lower and repeat on the other side.

Dumbbell Bicep Curl

Stand with palms forward. Keeping elbows at your sides, curl weights toward shoulders. Lower with control.

Superman T

Lie facedown with arms out to sides ('T' shape). Squeeze back and glutes to lift chest and arms off the floor.

Dumbbell Hammer Curl

Perform a bicep curl with palms facing each other (neutral grip).

Bird Dog

On all fours, extend one arm forward and the opposite leg back. Keep back flat. Return and switch sides.

Russian Twist

Sit leaning back with knees bent. Twist your torso to touch your hands to the floor on each side.

Jump Rope: Boxer Skip

Shift weight from foot to foot with each rope turn, like a boxer's shuffle.

Day 4: HIIT & Active Mobility

Boost your heart rate and improve movement quality.

HIIT Circuit

Perform each exercise for 30 seconds, followed by 15 seconds of rest. Complete 5-8 rounds of the entire circuit.

  • Jump Rope: Run in Place
  • Bodyweight Burpee
  • Mountain Climbers
  • High Knees

Mobility Flow

After your HIIT circuit, perform this gentle flow to aid recovery.

  • Cat-Cow (10 reps)
  • Thoracic Spine Windmills (8 each side)
  • World's Greatest Stretch (5 each side)

Day 5: Full-Body Metabolic Conditioning

Challenge your entire body as a single unit.

Exercise Instructions

Dumbbell Thruster

From a squat, use momentum to press dumbbells overhead as you stand up.

Dumbbell RDL

Hinge at your hips, keeping back straight and knees slightly bent. Lower dumbbells along your legs until you feel a hamstring stretch. Return to standing.

Plank Up-and-Downs

From a high plank, lower to a forearm plank one arm at a time, then push back up to a high plank.

Dumbbell Alternating Punches

In a fighting stance, punch one arm out at a time rapidly but with control.

Side-to-Side Hops

Hop with both feet from side to side over an imaginary line.

Static Cool-Down (7-10 Mins)

Perform this routine after every workout to improve flexibility and start the recovery process. Hold each stretch for 30 seconds per side.

Doorway Pectoralis Stretch: Stretch chest in a doorway.
Cross-Body Shoulder Stretch: Pull one arm across your body.
Overhead Triceps Stretch: Pull elbow of raised arm with other hand.
Standing Quad Stretch: Pull heel toward glute.
Seated Hamstring Stretch: Lean forward over one extended leg.
Kneeling Hip Flexor Stretch: Lunge forward to stretch hip.
Lying Figure-Four Stretch: Cross ankle over knee and pull thigh.
Standing Wall Calf Stretch: Lean against a wall with one foot back.
