The Core Advantage: The $7,500 Lease Credit

The most important factor making the Prologue lease so attractive is the **$7,500 Federal Clean Vehicle Credit**. Because of its assembly location, you can't get this credit if you buy the car. However, when you lease, Honda Financial Services claims a commercial credit and passes the full $7,500 directly to you as a capitalized cost reduction. This single incentive is the foundation of the exceptional lease value.

Understanding the Incentive Stack

Your final lease price is heavily influenced by stacking multiple incentives. Here’s a breakdown of the most common offers. Your eligibility for each will determine your total savings.

  • $7,500 Clean Vehicle Lease Credit: The foundational, non-negotiable credit applied to all Prologue leases through Honda Financial Services (HFS).
  • $1,000 - $3,500 Loyalty/Conquest Cash: An offer for current Honda owners (loyalty) or owners of competing brands (conquest). The amount varies by region and time.
  • $500 Military & Graduate Bonuses: HFS offers a $500 bonus for eligible military personnel and recent college graduates, which can be stacked with other offers.
  • Up to $2,000 Charge Up NJ Rebate: A point-of-sale rebate for NJ residents leasing from a participating NJ dealer. The amount is based on the vehicle's MSRP.
  • NJ ZEV Sales Tax Reduction: A major benefit where ZEVs are taxed at a reduced rate. This program is being phased out, making now a crucial time to act. As of late 2024, the rate is half the standard rate, but it is set to return to the full rate in July 2025.
  • Charger Rebates: NJ residents can also claim up to $250 for a Level 2 home charger, and utility companies like PSE&G offer additional credits.

Your Negotiation Playbook

Getting a great deal isn't automatic. Follow this proven strategy to ensure you secure the best possible terms on your Prologue lease.

Step 1

Do Your Homework

Before contacting a dealer, use online forums (like Edmunds) to find the current Residual Value (RV) and buy-rate Money Factor (MF) for your specific trim and term. Know all available incentives.

Step 2

Negotiate Price First

Focus only on the selling price (gross capitalized cost) of the vehicle, as if you were paying cash. Agree on this number *before* discussing lease payments or incentives.

Step 3

Verify the Worksheet

Get an itemized lease worksheet. Check that the RV and MF match your research. If the MF is higher, it's marked up—negotiate it down to the buy-rate. Confirm all incentives are applied.

Step 4

Scrutinize Fees

Question every fee. The Acquisition Fee should be the standard HFS rate. Politely decline any optional add-ons like nitrogen tires or protection packages you don't want.
